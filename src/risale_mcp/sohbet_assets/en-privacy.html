Chat page: Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{DATE_EN}}

This page describes what data the chat page ({{HOST}}{{P}}) processes. On the chat page you converse with an AI that answers your questions from the works of Bediüzzaman Said Nursî. The answer is written by Anthropic's Claude model; it reads the works with the tools on this server, and the server checks the quotations against the text. The page is in a trial phase. Our principle: collect nothing our work does not need.

The Risale MCP connectors (MCP servers) and the other pages of the website are not covered by this policy; the website's privacy policy applies to them.

Who is responsible

Risale MCP: a volunteer, independent project, published by and under the responsibility of Fatih Ebrar İnan. Contact: destek@risalemcp.com

Account

To use the page you open an account. We store: your e-mail address; an irreversible hash of your password (never the password itself); when the account was opened and when you last signed in; the number of failed attempts.

Of the 6-digit code sent to verify your address only a hash and its times are stored; it is deleted when used or after a day at the latest. To limit abuse, the number of codes requested per hour from one network (IP) address is counted in the server's memory only; your IP address is not written down. The number of codes sent by the whole site in a day is kept without addresses.

E-mail

Verification and password-reset codes are sent through Scaleway's e-mail service (France, EU). Scaleway receives only your address and the text of the e-mail with the code. You are sent no other e-mail.

Your questions and the answers

Your conversation is not stored on the server; it stays in your own browser. The "Yeni konuşma" (new conversation) button deletes it from your browser too.

When you ask a question, the question, the earlier questions and answers of the same conversation and the texts the AI reads are sent to Anthropic (Claude API) to write the answer. Anthropic processes them under its commercial terms, under which data sent through the API is not used to train its models.

When the AI searches the works by meaning, only the search phrase is sent to Voyage AI, which turns its meaning into numbers; the server does not write the result to disk.

Please do not put personal or sensitive information in your questions.

Usage log

For each question we log: the time, your account, the model, the amount of text processed and produced (tokens), the approximate cost, how many times the AI consulted the works, how long the answer took, and the counts of the citation check (how many sources, how many were moved to their right place, how many could not be verified). The question and the answer are not in this log.

The log serves the daily question allowance per person, the page's budget limit and monitoring of the service; it is deleted after 90 days.

Feedback

You can rate an answer 👍 or 👎 and add a note. We store the time, your account, your rating, your note and the model. The question and the answer are sent only if you tick "Soruyu ve bu cevabı da gönder" (send the question and this answer too), and only for that answer; the box is not ticked by default.

The project team reads feedback to improve the answers and to choose test questions; it is deleted after one year.

Cookie and browser

When you sign in, a session cookie is set in your browser: it applies only to the {{P}} path, lasts 30 days and ends at once when you sign out ("Çıkış"); the server keeps only its hash. Your conversation and the mark of the feedback you gave stay in your browser's own storage (localStorage). The page uses no analytics, advertising tools or other cookies; the server keeps no access log, and your IP address is not recorded.

Sharing

Data is not sold and not used for advertising or profiling. It is shared with no one other than Anthropic, Scaleway and Voyage AI as described above; each receives only what is said there.

Hosting

The chat page runs on a server in Hetzner Online's Helsinki data center (Finland, EU) (Hetzner privacy policy). The domain's DNS records are at Cloudflare; traffic does not pass through Cloudflare.

Deleting your account and your rights

"Hesap › Hesabımı sil" (Account › Delete my account) deletes your account yourself. Your account, sessions and codes are deleted; your address is removed from your usage log rows and your feedback; your notes and any questions and answers you sent are deleted, only the ratings remain, anonymous.

To learn what data is held about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, write to destek@risalemcp.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with its date.
