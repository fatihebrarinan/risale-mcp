Kullanıcının kararı (2026-11-24): dizin metinleri (Anthropic, OpenAI) başvurular sonuçlanınca değişir; bu taslak o
gün kullanılır. Bugünkü metin `docs/s4-dizin-basvurulari.md` › 2d ve OpenAI bölümündedir. Sunucu talimatı, site ve
eklenti skill'i E5.3'te değişti; dizin metni değişene kadar oradaki "beş eser" sözü eski kalır.

## Tek satır

> Read and cite the classical Arabic works the Risale-i Nur refers its reader to and relies on: rhetoric, kalam,
> usul and the Prophet's life, from al-Jurjani's Dala'il al-I'jaz to Sharh al-Mawaqif.

## Açıklama

> Classical Islamic Library gives Claude the classical Arabic works that Bediüzzaman Said Nursî's Risale-i Nur refers
> its reader to and relies on, in their original Arabic, together with the Qur'an's text:
>
> - Rhetoric, language and terms: 'Abd al-Qahir al-Jurjani's Dala'il al-I'jaz and Asrar al-Balagha, al-Sakkaki's
>   Miftah al-'Ulum, al-Raghib al-Isfahani's al-Mufradat, al-Sayyid al-Sharif al-Jurjani's al-Ta'rifat.
> - Kalam and legal theory: al-Sayyid al-Sharif's Sharh al-Mawaqif (with al-Iji's base text as a separate layer),
>   al-Taftazani's Sharh al-Maqasid and al-Talwih, Abu Hanifa's al-Fiqh al-Akbar, al-Ghazali's al-Munqidh.
> - The Prophet's life: al-Qastallani's al-Mawahib al-Laduniyya.
>
> For each work the server records where the Risale-i Nur names it and how (it refers the reader there, quotes it,
> or discusses it), by sentence id and citation; Claude reads those passages with the Risale-i Nur Library.
>
> Every sentence has a stable address in the sections of its edition:
>
> - Search by the works' own Arabic words and terms, or by meaning in any language.
> - Read a section or a passage in context and quote it exactly, with a numbered list of sources checked against
>   the text.
> - Find where a Qur'anic verse is quoted.
>
> Each work belongs to its author: editors' introductions and base texts are separate layers, and a link between a
> work and the Risale-i Nur is presented only as recorded, by its kind. Claude's own translations are labelled as AI
> translations.
>
> All tools are read-only and work without an account. The texts come from OpenITI (KITAB project; CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).
