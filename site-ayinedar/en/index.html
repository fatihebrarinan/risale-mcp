Âyinedâr

Ask the Risale-i Nur, the classical tafsirs, the hadith collections and the classical works; read the answer with its sources.

Âyinedâr is a free AI that answers your questions from the works of Bediüzzaman Said Nursî. You can also open the classical tafsirs, the hadith collections and the classical works the Risale-i Nur relies on. Every word it relies on is quoted from the source's own text; quotations are checked against the text and shown with their place in the work and their source.

What you can rely on

  • The texts come unchanged from sources that gave permission; every quotation is located down to its sentence.
  • Nothing the AI produces is called tafsir; interpretation is always kept apart from the text.
  • No ruling is given on hadith; grades are reported only as the books give them, with the scholar's name.
  • Free of charge, with no ads, no visitor tracking and no profit. A volunteer, independent service.

Sources and thanks

The texts are taken unchanged, with permission: e-Risale and risaleoku.com for the Risale-i Nur; OpenITI for the hadith collections, tafsirs and classical works; Tanzil for the text of the Qur'an. We thank all our sources. News and studies are published in Turkish: Yenilikler, Çalışmalar.
