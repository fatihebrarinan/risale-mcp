Âyinedâr

Around the Qur'an and the Sunna, ask the sources of the Islamic sciences, and read the answer with its sources.

Âyinedâr is a free AI that answers your questions from the sources of the Islamic sciences: the Qur'an, the hadith collections, the classical tafsirs, the complete Risale-i Nur and the classical works. An answer comes first with the verses and hadiths that bear on the question, then with the works that explain them. Every word it relies on is quoted from the source's own text, and quotations are checked against the text and shown with their place in the work and their source.

If you read the Risale-i Nur

The whole Risale-i Nur is here: all the works of Bediüzzaman Said Nursî, those he wrote in Arabic with their Turkish translations, every quotation cited down to its sentence. For an answer from the Risale-i Nur alone, choose the "Risale-i Nur'dan" mode in the menu above the question box, and your choice is remembered. For the places where a topic is treated across the works, there is the "Risale-i Nur'da konu taraması" mode.

What you can rely on

  • The texts come unchanged from sources that gave permission; every quotation is located down to its sentence.
  • Nothing the AI produces is called tafsir; interpretation is always kept apart from the text.
  • No ruling is given on hadith; grades are reported only as the books give them, with the scholar's name.
  • No legal ruling (fiqh) is given. To a question asking for a ruling, it shows what the books say in general and leaves the decision to a qualified scholar.
  • Free of charge, with no ads, no visitor tracking and no profit. A volunteer, independent service.
