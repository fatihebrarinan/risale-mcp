Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This page describes what the Âyinedâr root site (ayinedar.org: home page, news, studies and documents) processes. Our principle: collect nothing we do not need. The Turkish version is authoritative.

Responsible

What this site processes

This site consists only of pages to read; there are no accounts, forms or comments. The server keeps no access log; your IP address and the pages you read are not recorded. No cookies, visitor counters, advertising tools, or fonts and scripts loaded from other sites are used: every part of the page comes from our own server. If you choose light or dark mode with the button, the choice is stored only in your own browser and is not sent to the server.
