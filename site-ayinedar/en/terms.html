These terms are for the Âyinedâr root site (ayinedar.org). Each service has its own terms: the chat, the connectors. The Turkish version is authoritative.

Service

Âyinedâr is a volunteer, independent service that lets AI read and quote the Qur'an, the hadith collections, classical tafsirs, the Risale-i Nur and classical Islamic works while staying tied to the sources. It is free, carries no ads and has no commercial purpose.

AI answers and drafts

Answers and drafts are written by AI. Quotations are checked against the texts, but the AI's summary, interpretation or choice of passage may be wrong. Nothing the AI produces is tafsir or a fatwa, replaces a religious ruling, or is attributed to the author. On an important matter, read the quotation and its context in the work itself.
