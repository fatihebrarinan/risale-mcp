Classical Works

The Classical Works connector opens classical Arabic works on rhetoric (balagha), language and terms to AI assistants. The books of the masters of rhetoric that the Isharat al-I'jaz names are shown from their own text, with the work and the section.

Works

In order of their authors' deaths: 'Abd al-Qahir al-Jurjani, Dala'il al-I'jaz and Asrar al-Balagha · al-Raghib al-Isfahani, al-Mufradat fi Gharib al-Qur'an · al-Sakkaki, Miftah al-'Ulum · al-Sayyid al-Sharif al-Jurjani, al-Ta'rifat. Works of theology, Sufism and the Prophet's life will follow.

How it works

  • The unit of citation is the sentence. Sections are the edition's headings; the Dala'il is divided into its editor Mahmud Muhammad Shakir's numbered paragraphs, al-Mufradat into roots, al-Ta'rifat into chapters of the alphabet.
  • Texts are in their original Arabic. If you do not read Arabic, the assistant adds its own translation after the quotation and says it is an AI translation, not a published one.
  • The editor's introduction (Shakir's, in the Dala'il) is a separate layer, never attributed to the author.
  • The Isharat al-I'jaz names al-Jurjani and al-Sakkaki, but which passage it relies on is not recorded. A relation the assistant notices is its interpretation and stands under its own heading.
  • The text of the Risale-i Nur is not on this connector. Add the Risale-i Nur connector too and the assistant reads it there.
