The service

Risale-i Nur MCP is a connector (MCP server) that gives AI assistants the works of Bediüzzaman Said Nursî and the text of the Qur'an in a form they can read and cite down to the sentence. It is run by volunteers under the name Nur Hadimleri. It is free of charge, carries no advertising and has no commercial purpose.

AI answers

Answers are written by the AI assistant you use, not by the connector. The connector gives the assistant the text and the references and checks its quotations; even so, the assistant's summary, interpretation or choice of passage may be wrong. On an important matter, check the quotation and its context in the work itself. Neither the service nor the assistant's answers are a religious ruling or fatwa.

The translations of verses and hadiths in the works are the publishers', not the author's. Translations of the Qur'an do not take the place of the Qur'an.
