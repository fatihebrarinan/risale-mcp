Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Risale-i Nur MCP is a connector (MCP server) that gives AI assistants such as Claude and ChatGPT access to the works of Bediüzzaman Said Nursî. This page describes what data the four connectors, Risale-i Nur (mcp.risalemcp.com/mcp), Hadith (hadis.risalemcp.com/mcp), Tafsir (tefsir.risalemcp.com/mcp) and Classical Works (klasik.risalemcp.com/mcp), and this website (risalemcp.com) process. The same rules apply to all of them. Our principle: collect nothing we do not need to do the job.

Who is responsible

Risale MCP, a volunteer, independent project (risalemcp.com), published and run by Fatih Ebrar İnan. Contact: destek@risalemcp.com

Data the connector processes

Tool calls

When the assistant uses one of the connector's tools in your conversation, the server receives only that tool's input: a search phrase, a section or sentence id, or the words quoted for a citation. The rest of your conversation never reaches the server. These inputs are processed to produce the result and (except for the debugging exception below) are not stored.

Usage log

To keep the service running and to limit abuse, the following is logged for each request:

  • the time,
  • which connector, and the tool or request type,
  • how long the request took and, if it failed, the type of error.

Search phrases, quotations and conversation content are not logged. Log entries are deleted after 90 days.

The one exception is debugging: the people who run the service use a secret address of their own to see how the connector is used in chat applications. Only for requests to that address are the tool inputs, result sizes and error messages kept, for 7 days. Your requests go to the public address and never enter this record.

IP addresses and cookies

The server keeps no access log; your IP address is not recorded. Usage limits apply to all use of each connector together, not per person. The connector and the public pages of this website use no cookies, analytics or advertising tools; only the invitation-only working area described below uses a session cookie. If you choose the light or dark theme with the button, that choice is stored only in your own browser and never sent to the server.

Search by meaning

For search by meaning (semantic_search), the search phrase is sent to Voyage AI, which turns its meaning into numbers. Only the phrase is sent, not your conversation. The server keeps the result in memory while it runs and never writes it to disk. How Voyage AI handles data is described in its privacy policy.

Accounts

The connector needs no account and has no sign-in page; no user name or password is collected from anyone.

Invitation-only areas

The risalemcp.com/oku/ (reading platform) and risalemcp.com/taslak/ (working desk) areas are pages for reading unpublished AI drafts, open only to invited people; they are not public. An account is opened with an invitation link: the administrator sends it to you from their own e-mail (the site sends no e-mail), and you set your password yourself. The link works once and for 7 days; if you forget your password, you ask the administrator for a new link. With some links you type your e-mail address yourself; it is not verified, because it is only your sign-in name and no e-mail is sent to you.

For these areas we store: your e-mail address; an irreversible hash of your password (never the password itself); the number of failed attempts and the time of your last sign-in; a short note the administrator may add to recognise the account (e.g. your name; only the administrator sees it); only the hash and the times of the invitation link and of the session cookie set in your browser when you sign in; when you saw the welcome page; where you stopped reading (unit and paragraph, so that you can continue from there). A session lasts 30 days and ends at once when you sign out. Your IP address and the pages you view are not recorded.

Your notes. The notes you leave on the reading platform, and the words you mark, are stored on the server, tied to your account, so you also see them when you sign in from another device. Only you see your notes; they are not shared with anyone and are not used to revise the drafts. You can edit or delete a note at any time; a deleted note is deleted from the server too. On the "My notes" page you can download all of them as a Word file. Deleting your account deletes your notes and all your records in these areas.

The working desk is open only to the project team; reading decisions made there are stored on the server under a number, without your name, and marks put on the page stay in your browser.

Sharing

Data is not sold and not used for advertising or profiling. It is shared with no one other than Voyage AI as described above. The chat application you use (e.g. Claude, ChatGPT) processes your conversation under its own policy: for Claude, Anthropic's privacy policy applies, for ChatGPT, OpenAI's.

Hosting

The Risale-i Nur and Hadith connectors and this website run in Hetzner Online's Helsinki data center (Finland, EU), the Tafsir and Classical Works connectors in its Falkenstein data center (Germany, EU) (Hetzner privacy policy). Their requests are passed from the Helsinki server to Falkenstein over our own encrypted network (Tailscale, WireGuard); only the tool's input and result are passed. The domain's DNS records are at Cloudflare; traffic does not pass through Cloudflare.

Your rights

To learn what data is held about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, write to destek@risalemcp.com. The usage record cannot be linked to a person, so there is no personal data to give; the only exception is the accounts of the invitation-only working area.
