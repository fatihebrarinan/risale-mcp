Report a mistake

We work every day to make the connectors more accurate. If you notice a wrong quotation, a wrong reference or a missing work in an answer, write to us. We check every report against the source and fix it.

What you can report

  • A quotation or reference error: the words the AI shows are not at the place in the bibliography, or are tied to the wrong section.
  • A text error: a typo, a missing paragraph or a wrong heading in the text of the works.
  • A missing work or book: a work, hadith collection or tafsir you would like to see added.
  • A suggestion: anything that would make the connectors more useful.

How to write

A short e-mail is enough: destek@risalemcp.com. If you are reporting a mistake in an answer, include your question, the wrong part and the line from the bibliography; that helps us find it. You can also send the conversation's share link.
