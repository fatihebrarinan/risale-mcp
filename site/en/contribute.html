Contribute

Risale-i Nur MCP is looking for volunteers to review texts and the links between them and their sources.

Who is needed?

People who:

  1. read Arabic and can recognise the verse quotations in works of tafsir,
  2. read Arabic, understand the science of hadith and can recognise the hadiths in works of tafsir,
  3. know the methodology of tafsir and can see the basic connections between works of tafsir,
  4. know the Risale-i Nur well and can compare the sources given with the source in their own hands.

How will it work?

How the reviewing will be done is not settled yet; the platform it needs is being prepared. This page will be updated when it is ready.
