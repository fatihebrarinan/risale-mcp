Risale-i Nur

The Risale-i Nur connector opens all the works of Bediüzzaman Said Nursî to the AI you use. The AI reads the text before it speaks, quotes each passage from the work itself and shows its place down to the sentence. The texts are in Turkish (Ottoman Turkish), with the works written in Arabic in both languages.

Works

The Risale-i Nur Collection (the Words, the Letters, the Flashes, the Rays, the Supplements, the Biography and others), the early works (those written in Arabic in both Arabic and Turkish), the compilations (Asâ-yı Mûsâ, Zülfikâr, Sirâcü'n-Nûr and others) and the Hizbs: 53 works, about 131,000 sentences. In addition, 16 volumes in Ottoman script and the Qur'an with its vocalised text and two Diyanet translations. The full list is in the documentation.

How it works

  • Ask in today's words. Ask about "despair" and the AI also finds the place where the text says "ye's". Searching for an exact word, name or term works too.
  • Every quotation rests on a sentence. The answer ends with a list of sources: work, section and the first words of the quoted sentence. You can find that sentence in the book yourself.
  • Text and interpretation are kept apart. What the work says is under one heading, the AI's interpretation under another.
  • Translations of verses and hadith belong to the publishers. They were added later; the AI does not present them as Bediüzzaman's words.
  • Together with the other connectors. Add the Hadith and Tafsir connectors too and you see a hadith's source or a verse's place in the classical tafsirs in the same conversation.

Test question

Where in the Risale-i Nur is the parable of the man given twenty-four gold coins, explaining the value of the prayer, and what does it teach?

The answer should come with quotations from the Fourth Word and end with a list of sources. The difference between an answer with and without the connector is shown on the home page.

Sources

The texts are taken unchanged from sources that gave their permission: e-Risale, risaleoku.com (Risale-i Nur Library), the Keşif Yolculukları Risale-i Nur education programme and nurpedia.org. The Qur'an text comes from Tanzil, the translations from the Presidency of Religious Affairs (Diyanet) and the Türkiye Diyanet Foundation. Details in the documentation.
