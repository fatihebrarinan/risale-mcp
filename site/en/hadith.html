Hadith

The Hadith connector opens eighteen hadith collections to AI assistants. The unit of citation is the report: the assistant shows a report with its book, the edition's number and the printed page.

Books

The Six Books (al-Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, al-Tirmidhi, al-Nasa'i, Ibn Majah) and the Muwatta · Ahmad b. Hanbal, al-Musnad · al-Darimi, al-Sunan · al-Tabarani, al-Mu'jam al-Saghir · al-Hakim, al-Mustadrak (with al-Dhahabi's notes) · Ibn Hibban, al-Ihsan · al-Haythami, Majma' al-Zawa'id · Kanz al-'Ummal · al-Munawi, Fayd al-Qadir · al-'Ajluni, Kashf al-Khafa · Qadi 'Iyad, al-Shifa · al-Bayhaqi, Dala'il al-Nubuwwa.

How it works

  • Search by Arabic words or by meaning (in any language); go from a reference such as "Buharî, Ezân: 148" or "Müsned 14870" to the report; browse the books and chapters.
  • No rulings: the assistant never decides whether a report is sound or weak. Grades are shown only as the source gives them, with the scholar's name. Notes by al-Dhahabi, al-Albani and the editors are a separate layer, never attributed to the book's author.
  • A reference's volume and page may belong to another edition; the assistant says so. In books without numbers (al-Shifa, Dala'il) the id is this library's sequence and is marked as such.
  • The places in the Risale-i Nur where a report occurs are given by name and citation; their text is on the Risale-i Nur connector.
  • Texts are in their original Arabic; the assistant's translation is labelled an AI translation.

Test question

In which books does the saying "the disagreement of my community is a mercy" occur, and what is said about it? With sources, without a ruling.

Add to Claude
  • Claude (claude.ai, desktop, mobile): the button opens the add dialog with the name and address filled in. By hand: Customize › Connectors › Add › Add custom connector, name "Hadis", the address above. Steps and screenshots on the Claude page; only the name and address differ.
  • ChatGPT: Apps › Add › Add custom MCP server, name "Hadis", the server URL above, No authentication. Steps on the ChatGPT page.
  • Claude Code: claude mcp add --transport http hadis https://mcp.risalemcp.com/hadis

No account, free of charge. The Risale-i Nur, Hadith and Tafsir connectors are independent; add all three and the assistant gives each one's sources under its own name, in a single bibliography.
